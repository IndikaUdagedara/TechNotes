ESP32 wiki - 2-WAY 18650 Lithium Battery Shield V8 (the one I own; brand baishundianzi/Baishun; AliExpress item 1005008131562459, https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005008131562459.html). Listing/photo facts: holds 2x 18650 cells (the 1WAY/4WAY variants hold 1 or 4); board 100.3 x 48 mm, 39.41 g; outputs 5V/3A and 3V/1A (listing headline figures); header pads for 5V+GND along one long edge and 3V+GND along the other (several pairs each, solder-on), plus a USB-A output socket; charging input via Micro USB and USB-C (both on the back); 4 status LEDs; slide switch labelled HOLD / NORMAL; silkscreen warns to be careful of cell polarity (+ and -). Back-side parts visible in photos: boost converter with a 1R0 (1uH) inductor, a QFN controller IC (U2), an SOT-223 regulator that looks like an AMS1117 (U1, probably the 3V/3.3V rail), a K1 button/switch footprint and an unpopulated J4 2-pin footprint. Review findings from a 2-WAY buyer: cells held firmly and board well soldered; 5V output stable with no fluctuation; HOLD/NORMAL switch keeps power on even when load current drops very low (about 10mA), but the board does NOT repower an ESP that has gone into deep sleep (load too small to keep the converter running); the 4 LEDs show charge status but stay on while powering a load, with no way to turn them off, so they are a constant drain. Practical notes for ESP32: use the 5V pads/USB-A for dev boards, the 3V pad for bare 3.3V-ish loads; put HOLD mode on for low-current deep-sleep projects but test wake-up behaviour first; for battery-powered sleep-heavy builds consider desoldering the LEDs. Unknown from the listing (check on the board): charge current, cell protection, whether the two cells are in parallel, exact controller/regulator part numbers.
